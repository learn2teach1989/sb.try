🔒 Privacy Policy

SB Treasury Tool — TN Employee & Pensioners Tool | Last Updated: September 2026

SB Treasury Tool ("we", "the App", "this Tool") is a free reference and calculation tool built for Tamil Nadu Treasury employees and pensioners, covering IT Statement, TAPS, NHIS, Pension Plus/Minus Reconciliation and related utilities. This page explains what limited information the App collects, why, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

To identify who is using the tool and to allow you to contact us, we collect the following when you use the App:

DataWhen CollectedPurpose
Email IDAt LoginTo identify usage and allow access to the tool
Login Date & Time, Visit CountAt LoginUsage statistics for the tool owner
Device / Browser Type (User Agent)At LoginTo understand what devices are used, for compatibility
Name, Email, District, Feedback MessageOnly if you use "Contact / Feedback"To respond to your queries and improve the tool

We do NOT collect or store: your Date of Birth (used only at login to verify the format, never saved), passwords, financial account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, or any other sensitive government ID beyond what you voluntarily type into the tool's calculators for generating your own reports (this data stays on your device / is used only to generate the file you download — it is not transmitted to us).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To identify approximately how many people use the tool and how often (usage analytics).
  • To respond to feedback, questions, or bug reports you submit.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Login and feedback data is stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the tool's developer/owner. We use Google's infrastructure (Google Sheets + Google Apps Script) to store this information securely.

4. Third-Party Services

This App does not show any advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or tracking networks. It uses Google Apps Script only as a private data-logging mechanism controlled by the developer.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to use the "Contact / Feedback" feature if you do not wish to share your name, email, or district. Login with a valid email is required to access the tool's calculators.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by Tamil Nadu Government Treasury employees and pensioners (adults) and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
